# DeepSeekMath / GRPO

## Quick read

Introduces GRPO. Sample G rollouts per prompt, use group mean as a zero-bias baseline, advantage of rollout i = (r_i - mean_r) / std_r. Drops the critic network. Now the default RL algorithm for any tool-use or reasoning task where reward is verifiable.

## Why it matters to us

The algorithm underneath every RL recipe we use. Memory-R1, Search-R1, mempol — all GRPO. Understanding its variance properties (group_size, filter_constant) is critical for stable training.

## Method in one paragraph

Per training step: sample group of G rollouts from current policy. Compute reward per rollout. Compute group mean and std. Advantage for rollout i = (r_i - mean) / std. Standard policy-gradient update with this advantage. No value network.

## Results in numbers

- GSM8K and MATH benchmark improvements (specific numbers not extracted)
- Demonstrated as the algorithm behind DeepSeek-R1's chain-of-thought training

## What they don't do

- Doesn't address how to construct reward for non-verifiable tasks (open-ended generation).
- Variance reduction beyond group mean is unaddressed.

## Open questions / followups
