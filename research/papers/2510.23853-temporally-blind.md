# Your LLM Agents are Temporally Blind

## Quick read

Constructs TicToc, a 76-scenario benchmark testing whether agents correctly decide to refresh stale context vs. trust cached data. Best frontier model alignment with human preferences: only 65%. Concludes prompt engineering has limited effectiveness — this is an architectural deficit.

## Why it matters to us

Direct evidence for our "LLMs can't feel time" thesis. The 65% ceiling number is concrete. Their pairwise human-preference protocol is the right shape for behavioral benchmarks (better than 0/1 accuracy for cases where reasonable humans might disagree).

## Method in one paragraph

Scenarios are multi-turn dialogues where the agent has access to a tool with stale-able output. Pairs of agent responses are scored by humans for which is preferable given the implicit elapsed time. Alignment = fraction of pairs where agent matches human choice.

## Results in numbers

- Best model: 65% alignment with human preferences
- Prompt-engineering interventions: marginal improvements
- Naive scenario performance significantly worse

## What they don't do

- Only one axis of temporal behavior (refresh-vs-trust)
- Doesn't test deadline tracking, gap-aware resumption, staleness detection across multiple axes
- No architectural fix proposed
