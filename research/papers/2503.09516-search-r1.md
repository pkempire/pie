# Search-R1: Training LLMs to Reason and Leverage Search Engines with Reinforcement Learning

## Quick read

GRPO-trained search agent. Multi-turn search-then-answer over Wikipedia, reward = final answer correctness. +41% over RAG baselines on NaturalQuestions with Qwen2.5-7B. The structural template for any modern RL-for-tool-use recipe; we forked it for mempol.

## Why it matters to us

Our env/training scaffolding is a fork of theirs. Understanding what they did right (retrieved-token masking, GRPO config, connection pooling) is critical. Differences from us: they have single static corpus + one tool; we have per-conversation backend + 4-8 ops. They have read-only retrieval; we have write side.

## Method in one paragraph

Per question: agent emits search queries (a tool call), gets retrieved chunks, optionally emits more searches, then writes final answer. GRPO with group_size rollouts per question. Reward = final answer F1/EM vs gold. Retrieved tokens are masked during loss computation (don't backprop through Wikipedia content).

## Results in numbers

- +41% over RAG on NaturalQuestions with Qwen2.5-7B
- Multi-hop (HotpotQA, 2WikiMultihopQA) results positive but smaller
- Number scales with model size

## What they don't do

- No write side — corpus is static.
- Single tool only.
- Trajectory-level reward — no per-step credit assignment.
- Wikipedia-only — single domain.

## Open questions / followups

- Why retrieved-token masking? Worth understanding the gradient implications.
- How does it scale to N tools instead of 1?
