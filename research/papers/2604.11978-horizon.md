# HORIZON: Diagnosing Where and Why Agentic Systems Break

## Quick read

Dawn Song's group at Berkeley + Wisconsin. Cross-domain benchmark *purpose-built for long-horizon failure attribution*. 3100+ trajectories across 4 agentic domains, evaluated on GPT-5 variants + Claude models. The LLM-as-Judge pipeline is validated against human annotation at κ=0.84 — strong agreement. Public leaderboard at xwang2775.github.io/horizon-leaderboard, open for community submissions.

## Why this matters strategically

This is the **commercialization-anchor eval** we've been missing. LoCoMo measures one specific thing (multi-session conversational QA). HORIZON measures failure modes across 4 agentic domains. A system that lands top-3 on HORIZON gets attention from anyone building production long-horizon agents (Cursor, Devin, Claude Code, Decagon, Sierra).

For [[01-gepa-consolidator-on-locomo|Goal 01]]: the natural extension after LoCoMo is HORIZON. The consolidator+GEPA architecture, if it works on LoCoMo, should transfer to HORIZON's broader cross-domain shape — same primitives (raw log → consolidated state → answer), just more domains.
