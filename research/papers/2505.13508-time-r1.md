# Time-R1: Towards Comprehensive Temporal Reasoning in LLMs

## Quick read

A 3B model trained with a 3-stage RL curriculum (comprehension → prediction → generation) on a decade of timestamped news **beats 671B DeepSeek-R1 on future-event prediction**. Reasoning-over-time is a trainable skill, and a small specialized model can dominate a giant generalist on it.

## Why it matters to us

The cleanest "you can train the temporal skill" datapoint. Belongs to the reasoning-over-time thread (date arithmetic, event ordering, extrapolation) — NOT the awareness thread. Good contrast partner to Real-Time Deadlines (which shows the awareness gap can't be trained away the same way, only surfaced).
