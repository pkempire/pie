# RGMem

## Quick read

Hierarchical memory with renormalization-group-style coarse-graining: facts at the bottom, abstract preferences at the top, RG-flow-like operators between levels. 86.17% on LoCoMo (~7pp below current SOTA). Physics-inspired framing is the differentiator.
