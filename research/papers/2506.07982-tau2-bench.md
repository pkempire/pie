# τ²-Bench: Evaluating Conversational Agents in a Dual-Control Environment

## Quick read

Sierra Research's customer-service agent eval. Dual-control formalism: both the agent and the user can modify shared state through tools. 38 models on the leaderboard as of April 2026. Used by Sierra (Bret Taylor's company) as their primary production eval.

## Why it matters to us

The industry-applied benchmark. If we pitch contract-based orchestration to enterprise customers, τ²-Bench is what they'll ask about. The dual-control formalism transfers cleanly: replace "user" with "co-orchestrator agent" and the contract protocol applies.

## Method in one paragraph

Dec-POMDP with two action spaces (agent's tools, user's tools), shared state. Tasks framed as customer-service interactions (telecom domain primary, others available). Success = state reaches a desired terminal configuration.

Leaderboard at artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/tau2-bench. Specific numbers not extracted; need to fill in.

## What they don't do

- No multi-agent (>2 actors) formalism.
- No deadline/budget constraints.
- No mid-task interrupt-and-replan testing.
