# MultiAgentBench: Evaluating the Collaboration and Competition of LLM Agents

## Quick read

Canonical multi-agent eval at ACL 2025. Tests star, chain, tree, and graph coordination topologies with milestone-based KPIs (not just binary completion). Includes both collaboration tasks and competition tasks. The benchmark to report if making academic multi-agent claims.

## Why it matters to us

If we want academic credibility for the contract/checkpoint orchestration thesis, this is the eval to report against. The topology coverage maps cleanly to our delegation patterns.

## Method in one paragraph

Multi-task suite with explicit topology constraints (e.g. star = one orchestrator + N workers; tree = hierarchical delegation). Each task has milestone checkpoints scored independently — so partial collaboration credit is possible. Comparison across collaboration vs competition modes.
