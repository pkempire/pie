# Zep: A Temporal Knowledge Graph Architecture for Agent Memory

## Quick read

Bi-temporal KG memory: every edge has valid-time (when the fact was true) AND ingestion-time (when we learned it). Edges can expire. Open-source implementation as Graphiti. Closest existing thing to time-aware memory primitives.

## Why it matters to us

Their bi-temporal model is the right starting point for time-aware memory work. We'd extend it with (a) derivation propagation (JTMS-style), (b) belief revision (AGM-style), (c) learned validity-rule selection. Their substrate solves part of the time problem; we'd solve more of it.

## Method in one paragraph

Knowledge graph over entities and edges. Edges carry (valid_from, valid_until, ingested_at). Validity rules are hand-coded (e.g. employment edges expire when a new employment edge for the same person is added). Retrieval respects validity windows.

## Results in numbers

- LongMemEval: ~72% (varies)
- DMR (Deep Memory Retrieval): 94.8% (their own claim)
- The DMR number has been questioned in independent reproductions

## What they don't do

- No derivation graph — they store facts independently, not as derivations of other facts.
- No automatic retraction propagation when an upstream fact is invalidated.
- No belief revision when contradicting facts arrive simultaneously.
- Validity rules are hand-coded, not learned.

## Open questions / followups

- How robust is their DMR claim under independent re-evaluation?
- Could we build a derivation-tracked extension on top of Graphiti?
