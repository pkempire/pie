# Retrospex: Language Agent Meets Offline Reinforcement Learning Critic

## Quick read

Different angle on "learn from past experience" — instead of stuffing trajectories into memory, train an offline RL critic on the trajectory history. At decision time, combine the LLM's action likelihood with the critic's Q-value. Dynamic rescoring shifts weight toward the critic for interaction-heavy tasks.

## Why it matters to us

Not a competitor for consolidation work specifically, but a useful contrast: it answers "how do we use past experience" by training a Q-function instead of compressing into memory. Could be combined with a consolidator (critic over compressed bank). Probably out of scope for our first experiments.

## Method in one paragraph

Two scoring components per action: LLM likelihood P(a|s) and offline-RL Q(s,a). Final policy is a weighted combination; weighting is dynamic based on task type (more interaction-heavy → critic weighted higher). Critic is trained offline via standard offline-RL methods on a trajectory replay buffer.

## Results in numbers

Outperforms baselines on ScienceWorld, ALFWorld, Webshop. EMNLP 2024 main proceedings.

## Abstract (verbatim, for reference)

> Large Language Models (LLMs) possess extensive knowledge and commonsense reasoning capabilities, making them valuable for creating powerful agents. However, existing LLM agent frameworks have not fully utilized past experiences for improvement. This work introduces a new LLM-based agent framework called Retrospex, which addresses this challenge by analyzing past experiences in depth.
