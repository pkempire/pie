# Real-Time Deadlines Reveal Temporal Awareness Failures in LLM Strategic Dialogues

## Quick read

Put a real clock on a negotiation and LLMs that can't see elapsed wall-clock time close **4%** of deals; give them periodic remaining-time updates in the prompt and the same model closes **32%**. The capability is in the weights — the model just doesn't condition on time unless you hand it the clock every turn.

## Why it matters to us

This is the load-bearing citation for temporal **awareness** (≠ temporal reasoning). It says the cheapest possible fix — a `current_time` / `remaining_time` tag at every turn — buys a +28pp behavioral swing. Directly motivates adding a clock header to every mempol write/read observation, and is the strongest single number for the "behaving-in-time" half of the channel's temporal episode.
