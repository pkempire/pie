Two axes, one field

The vertical axis is where knowledge lives — as re-read text, as a pre-digested KV cache, or baked into weights. The horizontal axis is when you pay to organize it — up front at write-time, or on demand at read-time. The color is the catch: it marks what access you need to build each one — and it’s what explains the map’s lopsided shape.

Hover or tap any node for what it is, why it sits there, and its one real bottleneck.

What the colors reveal: the crowded token row is almost entirely green — it runs on any closed API, so anyone can build there, and everyone did. The cache row is red: it needs access to the model’s internals, so it’s nearly empty of products. The weights row is amber: it needs open weights to train, so it lives only in research. The field isn’t shaped like this by taste. It’s shaped by who’s allowed through the door — and the two open rows are where memory turns into genuine learning.
